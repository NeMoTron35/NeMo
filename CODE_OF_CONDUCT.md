# Community code of conduct

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant's principles of an open, respectful community.

We commit to a harassment-free experience regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, show empathy and focus on what benefits the community. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

These standards apply in project spaces and when representing Quantum QLi publicly. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or reject contributions. Report incidents privately to maintainers through an existing private contact channel; never expose a reporter's identity in a public issue. No dedicated conduct-reporting address is configured yet.

Enforcement should be proportional: correction, warning, temporary restriction or permanent exclusion. Maintainers must consider context, explain decisions when safe and protect confidentiality. This policy adapts [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/); the linked policy is not the project's source-code license.
