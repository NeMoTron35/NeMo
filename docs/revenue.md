# Revenue

Open `/fees` to inspect fee totals, collection information and exposed fee-claim actions. These values come from demo records and are not a live claimable wallet balance.

## Fee claim

Meteora DBC separates creator and partner fee claims. The service factory exposes creator/partner fee-claim helpers; the UI builds reviewed previews rather than silently sending them.

## Interpretation

Fee estimates, accounting periods and selected pools describe the demonstration. No yield guarantee, confirmed claim or actual revenue ledger is implied. Signing and submission remain separate concerns.
